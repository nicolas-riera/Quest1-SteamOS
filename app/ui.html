SteamOS for the Oculus Quest 1

Version . Replaces the headset's Android with SteamOS, Steam and SteamVR.

What you need

  • An Oculus Quest 1 updated to Meta's last software, with developer mode and USB debugging on.
  • An unlocked bootloader (unlocking it wipes the headset): see the project page.
  • A USB data cable, the headset charged to 50 % or more.
  • About 30 GB free on this PC while installing (another drive can be picked) and an internet connection (about 7 GB to download). Everything is deleted at the end.

What happens

The installer checks the headset, then downloads SteamOS and Steam from Valve, Meta's tracking software from Meta's last update and this project's files, builds the system on the headset (it works on files kept on this PC: nothing is written to the headset until the very end) and installs it. Nothing from Valve or Meta is shipped with this program. It takes about an hour; keep the headset plugged in.

Checking the headset

Plug the headset in. It restarts into its bootloader for a moment, then back into Android.

The headset will be wiped

Android and everything on the headset are erased: apps, games, saves, Meta account data. The headset will only run SteamOS. Meta's software can be put back later with Meta's update file (the installer's "back to stock" option, or by hand with fastboot).

The bootloader, the firmware and the headset's own calibration and tracking data are never touched.

Installing

Keep the headset plugged in and this window open.
